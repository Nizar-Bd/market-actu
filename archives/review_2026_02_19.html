The CAC 40 slipped 0.3% to 8,402 on Thursday, retreating from the record high reached in the previous session as market sentiment turned subdued amid mounting geopolitical tensions. US–Iran tensions escalated after President Donald Trump called handing over the Chagos Islands and leasing the military base a “big mistake,” stating that the US might use Diego Garcia against Iran if a nuclear deal fails. Elsewhere, the second day of Ukraine–Russia talks ended without a significant progress. Meanwhile, investors parsed the latest minutes from the Federal Reserve, which underscored divisions among policymakers over the interest-rate outlook. On the corporate front, Renault shares fell 1.4% after reporting a substantial net income loss of €10.9 billion in 2025, with CEO François Provost attributing the decline to a “challenging market environment.” Moreover, Airbus tumbled 5.3% after the planemaker trimmed its primary jet production target.

Frankfurt's DAX 40 fell 0.5% to near 25,150 on Thursday, down from one-month highs reached in the prior day. A cautious mood prevailed as quarterly earnings took center stage and elevated geopolitical risks lingered. Ongoing US-Iran tensions, with limited progress in nuclear talks, have raised concerns over potential military action. Meanwhile, the FOMC minutes showed officials deeply divided on interest rates, with several pointing to possible rate hikes if inflation stays high. Among top losers, Airbus tumbled more than 5% after the company lowered its main jet production target amid ongoing supply-chain constraints, despite reporting slightly higher Q4 profits. Autos also faced pressure, with Volkswagen, BMW and Mercedes-Benz Group falling 2.1%, 1.6% and 1.4%, respectively. On the upside, shares of Vonovia rose nearly 2% after Morgan Stanley upgraded the company to Equalweight from Underweight, marking the end of a two-year period in which the stock had been rated underweight.

European equities edged lower on Thursday, retreating slightly from record highs reached in the previous session.
