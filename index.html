commodity/eu-natural-gas 2026-01-26
https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/eu-natural-gas

European natural gas futures rose 6% to €42.4 per megawatt-hour, the highest since March 2025, as supply concerns intensified amid falling US LNG flows and rising winter demand. Gas flows to US liquefied natural gas export plants fell to the lowest in a year after winter storms disrupted production, limiting export availability just as Europe prepares for another deep freeze. The region relies heavily on LNG after losing most Russian pipeline supplies following the invasion of Ukraine, with LNG now covering roughly half of Europe’s gas needs. The EU sourced 27% of its total gas and LNG imports from the US in 2025, up from 6% in 2021, increasing exposure to US supply disruptions. At the same time, unusually cold weather is forecast to persist into early February, lifting heating demand. Gas storage is being drawn down rapidly, with EU inventories at 45.6% compared with 56.5% a year ago, including low levels in Germany (37.5%), France (36.4%), and the Netherlands (31.1%).

france/stock-market 2026-01-26
https://tradingeconomics.com/france/stock-market

The CAC 40 fell 0.3% to around 8,115 on Monday, extending the downward trend from last week, as global risk-off sentiment spread amid geopolitical tensions and trade uncertainties. Although Trump has withdrawn the Greenland tariff threat, he warned that Canada could face 100% tariffs if it strikes a trade deal with China, keeping investors wary about whether tariffs may become a standard tool in global trade. Danone led losses, falling 4.6% after recalling specific infant formula batches over cereulide concerns on Friday. Other notable decliners included STMicroelectronics (-1.5%), Airbus (-1.4%), Accor (-1.3%), and Essilor (-1.2%). On the upside, TotalEnergies SE rose 1.2% after reaching an agreement with the Libyan government to extend its oil and gas exploration concession in the Waha oil field until 2050, while adjusting financial terms to boost production. Bank stocks also edged higher, with Société Générale (+1.2%), Crédit Agricole (+0.6%), and BNP Paribas (+0.4%) seeing gains.
